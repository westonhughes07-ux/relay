How to play

60 seconds to learn
  • Tap a tile to rotate it 90°. Right-click or long-press rotates back.
  • Signal flows out of the bright square core through connected wire.
  • Light up every node to solve it. Every board is guaranteed solvable.
  • Scored like golf — par is the fewest possible turns. Beat par if you can.
  • Daily is the same board for everyone. Endless never runs out.
  • Hints cost +5 moves and lock one tile correct. Three per board.
