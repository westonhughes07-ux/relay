How the grid works

Ten seconds, then you're on your own
  • The bright box in the middle is the substation. Current flows out through connected conduit.
  • Tap any tile to turn it. Right-click or long-press turns it back.
  • Power every terminal to bring the block up. Every grid can be solved.
  • Scored like golf — par is the fewest turns possible. Beat it if you can.
  • Each grid you finish lights one more building on the skyline. It stays lit.
  • Stuck? A hint locks one tile right for +5 turns. Three per grid.
