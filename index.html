How To Play

It takes ten seconds!
  • Tap a tile to spin it. The glowing core in the middle is the power source.
  • Connect every wire so the whole grid lights up. Every puzzle can be solved!
  • Finish at or under par for 3 stars ⭐⭐⭐
  • Each puzzle you beat lights a building in your city — forever.
  • Stuck? A hint fixes one tile for +5 turns. You get 3.
